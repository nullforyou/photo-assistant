常见问题 / Common Questions

1. 相机/相册无法使用？

首次打开请允许「相机」「照片」权限。若曾拒绝，去 iPhone 设置 → Photo Assistant Studio 重新开启即可。

1. Camera/Photos not working? Allow Camera & Photos permission on first launch, or re-enable it in iPhone Settings → Photo Assistant Studio.

2. 照片保存在哪里？

拍照后点保存，照片会存入系统「照片」App，App 本身不上传、不收集任何数据，卸载即清除。

2. Where are photos saved? Tapping save stores the photo in the system Photos app. The app uploads and collects no data; uninstalling removes everything.

3. 支持哪些语言？

简体中文、繁体中文、英语、日语、韩语、法语、德语、泰语、葡萄牙语、西班牙语、土耳其语，共 11 种界面语言。

3. Languages? Simplified/Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Thai, Portuguese, Spanish, Turkish — 11 in total.

4. 想删除数据？

本 App 不持有任何用户数据，无需申请删除。如需其他帮助，发邮件给我们。

4. Data deletion? The app holds no user data, so no deletion request is needed. For anything else, email us.
